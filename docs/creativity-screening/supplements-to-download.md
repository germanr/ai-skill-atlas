# Supplements to download by hand

These papers have a supplement that the scripts could not fetch: the publisher's site refused scripted requests, and the paper is not in PubMed Central. Open the link, download the supplementary files, and save them in `D:\projects\ai-skill-atlas\creativity-supplements\<cid>\` (one folder per paper id). The extraction then adds what they print.

## Hold statistics the extraction needs

- **c161**, Mei et al. (2025), *Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans*: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2025.100140. SI Table 3 holds the pooled treatment effects across groups, which the main text does not print; arm sizes are not in the main text either.
- **c060**, Gordetzki et al. (2026), *Information Systems Research*: https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.2024.0952. Online Appendix D holds the descriptives; the main text gives no arm sizes, arm means, or SDs.
- **c072**, Jin et al. (2026), *Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans*: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100288. Table S2 holds the means and SDs; the main text prints them only for the AI arm.
- **c032**, Bangerl et al. (2025), CHI 2025: https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713886. The supplement holds descriptive statistics of the sample and may hold the SDs of the AUT outcomes that the main text omits.
